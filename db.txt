Now your Handyman app can connect to handyman_db using:

Host: localhost
Port: 5432
Database: handyman_db
Username: handyman_user
Password: securepassword

# Connect to your database
psql -U handyman_user -h localhost -d handyman_db

# Backup your database
pg_dump -U handyman_user -h localhost handyman_db > backup.sql

# Restore from backup
psql -U handyman_user -h localhost -d handyman_db < backup.sql
